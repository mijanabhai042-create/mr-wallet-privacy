Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

App Name: MR Wallet  •  Package: com.mr.wallet

MR Wallet ("we", "our", or "us") is a utility and currency rate tracking assistant designed to help users monitor rates, submit support inquiries, and manage transaction requests securely. We are committed to safeguarding user data and upholding transparent privacy practices.

Overview: MR Wallet manages minimal operational data needed to maintain session security. We do not access your personal device contacts, continuous background location, or external files outside of media manually selected for support attachments.

This Privacy Policy explains our data practices in full compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and applicable privacy regulations.

1. Information We Collect

We process limited operational data required to operate core features reliably:

Data Type Purpose Collection Point
Account & Device Identifiers
Anonymous device reference tokens or registered email identifiers.		 To authenticate session state and link your app instance securely with backend cloud storage. Generated automatically or provided upon login.
Support Messages & Attached Media
Text messages, inquiry reference details, and payment verification screenshots.		 To assist support admins in processing your inquiries and resolving transactional issues. Voluntarily submitted by you via the in-app chat interface.
Push Notification Tokens
Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push tokens.		 Used solely to deliver real-time support status notifications and urgent system announcements. Generated on app initial launch.
Local App Data
App configuration state, cached rates, and UI preferences.		 To ensure smooth performance and offline responsiveness. Stored locally in secure private app storage.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Sensitive banking credentials, credit card numbers, passwords, PINs, or OTP secrets.
  • Device contact lists, call history, SMS logs, or browser web histories.
  • Continuous background GPS tracking, microphone audio monitoring, or unprompted camera access.
  • Ad-tracking identifiers or user data for third-party advertising profiling.

2. Third-Party Infrastructure Services

We rely on trusted enterprise infrastructure services to process technical operations securely:

  • Google Firebase: We utilize Firebase Realtime Database for real-time message sync and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for push notifications. Technical telemetry is processed per Google’s privacy protocols.
  • Brevo (SMTP Service): Used strictly for transmitting operational email notifications (such as verification or support alerts) when requested.

3. How We Use Information

Collected data is used strictly for operational purposes:

  • Displaying real-time exchange rate updates and operational notices.
  • Enabling communication with support staff to resolve inquiry tickets.
  • Dispatching transactional push notifications regarding your active requests.
  • Detecting platform abuses, ensuring backend API security, and debugging app crashes.

4. Security & Data Protection

Your data security is enforced through modern protective standards:

  • Encryption in Transit: All web traffic and cloud transactions utilize mandatory TLS/SSL 256-bit encryption.
  • Access Restrictions: Database records and administrative logs are restricted to authorized support personnel only.
  • Local Isolation: Locally saved preferences are secured within Android's private app sandbox environment.

5. Android System Permissions

MR Wallet requests only essential permissions necessary for functionality:

Permission Purpose
INTERNET Required for fetching rate feeds, syncing support chat, and processing backend requests.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Monitors internet availability to notify users when working offline.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Delivers notifications when administrators reply to your inquiries. Can be toggled off in device settings.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion Requests

Temporary media attachments and chat logs are periodically purged after 60 days unless retained for active troubleshooting. Users retain full authority to request data removal.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

You may request the permanent removal of your backend data records at any time:

  1. Open MR Wallet, navigate to Settings, and copy your unique User ID.
  2. Email our support team with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" including your User ID.
  3. All associated database logs will be permanently deleted within 7 business days.

You may also instantly wipe local device settings by clearing app storage via Android System Settings.

7. Children's Privacy

MR Wallet is not directed toward children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. If you become aware that a minor has submitted personal data, please contact us for swift remediation.

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect app updates or regulatory changes. Revised terms will be posted directly to this URL with an updated effective date.
